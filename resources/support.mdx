## Getting Help

### For Bug Reports

When reporting bugs, please be descriptive and thorough. Include:

<Steps>
  <Step title="System Information">
    - macOS version
    - Ora Browser version
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reproduction Steps">
    Clear, numbered steps to reproduce the issue
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expected vs Actual Behavior">
    What you expected to happen and what actually happened
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supporting Materials">
    - Screenshots if applicable
    - Relevant console/error messages
  </Step>
</Steps>

### For Feature Requests

When requesting features, please provide:

- **Clear description** of the feature and its use case
- **Explanation** of how it fits with Ora's goals (fast, secure, beautiful)
- **Implementation considerations** including complexity and user impact
- **Research** to confirm similar functionality doesn't already exist

<Note>
Check the [roadmap](/resources/roadmap) to see if your feature is already planned.
</Note>
